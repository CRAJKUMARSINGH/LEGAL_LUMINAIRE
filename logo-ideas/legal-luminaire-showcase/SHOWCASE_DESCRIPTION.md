# Legal Luminaire: Accuracy-First Legal AI for Indian Advocates

## Overview
Legal Luminaire is an AI-powered legal drafting platform designed specifically for Indian advocates. It combines advanced AI technology with rigorous citation verification to help legal professionals produce accurate, well-cited legal documents while maintaining professional responsibility.

## Core Value Proposition
**AI legal drafting that blocks unsafe citations before filing.**

## Key Features

### 🔍 Citation Verification & Safety
- **Fact-Fit Gate** — Scores every precedent against the case facts, evidence and procedural context
- **Citation Verification** — Classifies authorities as Court-Safe, Verified, Secondary, Pending or Fatal Error
- **Hard Citation Block** — Prevents draft export while unresolved or unsafe citations remain
- **Verification Gate** — Five verification tiers expose risky citations before drafting

### 🤖 AI-Powered Research & Assistance
- **Grounded Legal Copilot** — Provides matter-specific research assistance with source-linked answers
- **AI Legal Research** — Searches precedents, statutes and engineering standards through a structured case workflow
- **Citation Research** — Research authorities without losing the factual context of the case

### 📝 Drafting & Document Creation
- **Drafting Engine** — Generate a structured legal draft from the selected matter
- **Bilingual Drafting** — Supports Hindi and English legal documents, including bail, discharge and written submissions
- **Pre-Filing Checklist** — Gives advocates a final human-review workflow before submitting a draft

### ⏰ Case Management & Organization
- **Chronology Studio** — Builds a source-cited timeline of facts, documents, hearings and events
- **Deadline Engine** — Tracks limitation periods, court vacations and important filing dates
- **Case Dashboard** — One workspace from intake to filing review
- **Case Intake** — Start with the FIR, charge-sheet or forensic report

### 📚 Knowledge & Standards
- **Standards Explorer** — Helps distinguish and apply relevant Indian, IS, ASTM and forensic standards
- **Accuracy Academy** — Demonstrates the risks and trade-offs of AI-assisted legal work
- **26 Synthetic Demo Cases** — Covers criminal, civil, NDPS, negotiable instruments, arbitration and infrastructure matters

## Target Users
- Indian advocates practicing in criminal, civil, and specialized courts
- Legal professionals requiring bilingual drafting capabilities
- Lawyers handling complex technical and infrastructure matters
- Advocates who prioritize accuracy and citation integrity
