## 7. Legal Accuracy and Safety

Because the project relates to legal matters, explain the safeguards incorporated into the tool.

Include, where relevant:

- Legal disclaimer stating that the tool does not replace advice from a qualified lawyer
- Jurisdiction covered, for example, India or a particular state
- Whether outputs are informational, educational, workflow-supporting, or advisory in nature
- Source or authority used for legal content, such as statutes, rules, judgments, official portals, or verified public materials
- Process for reviewing legal content for accuracy
- Limits on the tool’s use
- Clear escalation guidance for users who need professional legal advice
- Measures to avoid misleading, harmful, discriminatory, or overconfident AI outputs

### Suggested Disclaimer

> This tool is intended for informational and workflow-support purposes only. It does not constitute legal advice, does not create a lawyer-client relationship, and should not be relied on as a substitute for advice from a qualified legal professional.
