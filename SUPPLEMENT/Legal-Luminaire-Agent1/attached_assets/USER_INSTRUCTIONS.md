## Task 3: New Point — FSL Mortar/Plaster Result Is Absurd (ADD THIS POINT)

**Point to add (in Hindi, to be sandwiched):**

**Core argument:**
> 1:18 अनुपात के मोर्टार का प्लास्टर **माँकस्थल पर आज तक विद्यमान नहीं होता** — यह FSL का निष्कर्ष स्वयं ही तकनीकी दृष्टि से असंभव एवं हास्यास्पद है।

**Legal/technical development needed:**
1. 1:18 (cement:sand) mortar is far too weak / lean — never used in any construction specification
2. Standard plaster ratios are 1:3, 1:4, 1:6 — never 1:18
3. A structure plastered with 1:18 mortar would crumble immediately — it cannot survive 4 years
4. The FSL result of 1:18 is a direct consequence of testing *hardened* mortar with wrong standards (IS 1199:2018 — fresh concrete), combined with carbonation layer not removed
5. This absurd result itself proves the methodology was wrong
6. ASTM C1324 §§7–8 require removal of 5–10mm carbonated layer before analysis — failure to do so artificially dilutes cement content, giving false lean mix ratio
7. Cross-link with IS 2250:1981 — minimum cement content for external plaster is 1:6
8. Argue: no contractor, engineer, or PWD specification has ever prescribed 1:18 mortar for plastering — this alone renders the FSL report perverse and self-refuting
9. Cite: if the court accepts a 1:18 mortar ratio, it would mean the building stood for 4 years with mortar that has no binding strength — an engineering impossibility
