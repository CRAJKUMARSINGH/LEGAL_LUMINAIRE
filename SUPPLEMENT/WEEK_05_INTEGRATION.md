# Week 05 — INTEGRATION

**Primary mission:** Regulatory/technology integration, adversarial QA and release readiness.

## Command brief
You are the named workstream owner. Implement these assigned examples as synthetic fixtures for Legal Luminaire. Do not treat them as legal advice or filing documents. Preserve headings 0–4 exactly. Verify current Indian law, court rules, forms, limitation and citations before any real-world use.

## Mandatory operating orders
1. Retain example number/title and model approach status, urgency, missing evidence, forum uncertainty and matter state.
2. Mark every assumption and unresolved fact; never replace a blank with a plausible invention.
3. For each draft, record jurisdiction, current-law verification, deadline state, annexures, approval owner and template status.
4. Add an adverse or contradictory fact and prove the workflow does not silently discard it.
5. Pending or unverified citation/deadline data blocks any filing-ready label.
6. Use synthetic names, amounts and identifiers only; never import client data.
7. Submit changed files, validation evidence, open questions and one next action.

## Definition of done
- [ ] Sections 0–4 and approach status render.
- [ ] Missing documents produce a gap list.
- [ ] Unclear, overbroad or unsafe requests are representable.
- [ ] Action plan separates facts, research, drafting, review and filing.
- [ ] Drafts distinguish notice, pleading, application, affidavit, deed, checklist and no-filing note.
- [ ] Citation and limitation gates are visible.
- [ ] No fixture is filing-ready without supervising-advocate approval.
- [ ] Existing accuracy, protected-file and test-asset conventions remain intact.

## Implementation tasks
### A. Fixture and schema
- Use the repository's existing synthetic-input/test-asset convention.
- Add deterministic IDs, domain, complexity, approach status, urgency, document completeness and workflow state.

### B. Draft-state behaviour
- Use safe states: intake-only, research-needed, draft, review-needed, ready-for-supervising-advocate, blocked.
- Explain blocked states; do not merely disable buttons.
- Keep generated templates distinct from completed instruments.

### C. Quality evidence
- Assert numbering, headings, status, unresolved facts and no fabricated citations.
- Test one happy, one incomplete-document and one contradictory-fact path.
- Record commands and outputs in the handoff.

### D. Handoff
- Report files, schema decisions, unresolved questions, test evidence and rollback-safe notes.
- Do not rewrite another agent's protected work without narrow ownership.
