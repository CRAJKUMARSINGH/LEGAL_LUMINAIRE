# Legal Luminaire — 51 Drafting Intake Examples

This supplement adds 51 original synthetic training examples for the intake-to-drafting workflow. They cover incomplete papers, uncertain remedies, urgent deadlines, conflicting evidence, vulnerable clients and matters that should not be drafted yet.

## Source and scope
The examples are an original educational adaptation of topic areas in the University of Delhi Faculty of Law PDF [Drafting, Pleadings and Conveyancing](https://lawfaculty.du.ac.in/userfiles/downloads/LLBCM/Drafting-Pleadings-and-Conveyancing.pdf). They do not reproduce the book's forms or prose. Verify all Indian statutes, procedural codes, court rules, forms, limitation periods and citations as current before use.

**Educational disclaimer:** Synthetic fixtures only—not legal advice, filing pleadings, or a substitute for conflict checks, professional judgment, client consent and court-specific review.

## Required five-part schema
Every example retains: **0. Background**, **1. Data and documents**, **2. Request**, **3. Action plan**, and **4. Drafts created**, with approach status and draft state.

## Professor grading rubric
Score 0–2 for: five-part fidelity; approach variation; evidence realism; action sequencing; honest draft state; current-law/citation caution; deadline awareness; privacy/safety; contradiction handling; handoff quality. Fail a fixture if it invents a material fact, hides a gap, calls a draft filing-ready without review or bypasses an accuracy gate.
